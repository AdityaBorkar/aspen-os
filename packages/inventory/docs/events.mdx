title: Events
description: Events produced and consumed by the Inventory module.
icon: IconBell
---

Type-level contract passed through `ModuleInfra.events`; publishing goes
through `ctx.pubsub.publish` with camelCase payloads.

## Produced (`inventory.*`)

- `warehouse_created | _updated | _disabled`
- `stock_changed` — per ledger commit (`itemId`, `warehouseId`, `qtyDelta`,
  `valuationRate`, `voucherType/Id`, `isTransitLeg` so accounting can skip GL
  on transit legs); consumed by accounting for GL posting.
- `stock_entry_submitted | _cancelled` — the submitted event carries the
  additional-cost total for landed-cost booking.
- `reconciliation_submitted`
- `reorder_triggered` — breach details for accounting's Material Request flow.
- `reservation_created | _consumed | _released`
- `pick_list_created | _submitted | _cancelled`
- `putaway_applied` — split plan applied on submit.
- `serial_created | _delivered`
- `batch_created | _expired | _split | _moved`

## Consumed (`$consumes`, introspection-only)

- `products.item_created | _updated | _disabled` — movement validation and
  reorder defaults (snapshotted onto rows, never read directly).
- `products.item_price_created | _updated` — basic-rate snapshots.
- `masters.unit_of_measure_updated` — conversion-factor guard.
- `accounting.material_request_created | _cancelled`,
  `accounting.purchase_order_created`, `accounting.sales_order_created |
_updated | _closed | _cancelled`, `accounting.receipt_created`,
  `accounting.delivery_created` — fulfilment pointers that trigger or consume
  movements. Accounting owns `% supplied` updates off `stock_changed`.
