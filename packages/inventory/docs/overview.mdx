`@aspen-os/inventory` is the multi-warehouse stock-control module: a perpetual
append-only stock ledger with FIFO / moving-average valuation, stock entries
for every movement, reservations, putaway/pick execution, serial/batch
traceability, and physical-vs-book reconciliation.

All tables are tenant-scoped with the `inventory_` prefix. Product definitions,
prices, UOMs, and parties live in `@aspen-os/products`, pricelist data,
`masters`, and accounting — inventory keeps soft foreign keys plus rate
snapshots, never local masters.

## Concepts

- **Warehouse** — a storage node in a tree (`parent_id` + `is_group`). Only
  leaves hold stock; groups organize. Warehouses are disabled, never deleted,
  once they have history.
- **Stock ledger** — the immutable per-movement projection
  (`item × warehouse × qty × valuation_rate`). Every submitted movement appends
  rows; cancellations append counter-rows. Nothing is ever edited in place.
- **Valuation** — per-item method (`fifo | moving_average`, default from
  settings). Receipt rows carry the transaction rate; the moving average is
  derived (`value / qty`), never stored. Repack/manufacture rolls input cost
  into new items; transfers carry cost unchanged.
- **Available vs on-hand** — reservations hold `available = on_hand − reserved`
  without moving physical stock.
- **Freeze window** — `freeze_upto_date` / `freeze_older_than_days` block
  backdated postings except for `freeze_allowed_role`.

## Module shape

`Inventory.create(config?)` with `$name = "inventory"`,
`$dependencies = ["masters", "products"]`, and `$consumes` covering
`products.*`, `masters.unit_of_measure_updated`, and `accounting.*` fulfilment
topics (introspection-only). `$prepareRuntime` registers the nightly
`inventory.reorder-scan` cron; `$cleanup` unregisters it.

Config: `reorderScanCron` (default `30 2 * * *`).

## Explicit non-goals

Stock Reports/aging analytics, item/pricing masters, accounting fulfilment
documents, manufacturing execution, QC inspection, and landed-cost allocation
are out of scope — see the spec. The ledger exists for valuation and audit;
analytics belong to `workspace` dashboards.
