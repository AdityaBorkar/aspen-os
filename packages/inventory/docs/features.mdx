## Warehouses

- **Warehouse tree** — Organize rooms, shelves, and bins under group nodes; only leaves hold stock.
- **Warehouse types** — Stock, WIP, transit, supplier, customer, room, shelf, bin, plus custom types.
- **Transit moves** — Two-leg transfers stage through a transit warehouse.

## Stock movements

- **Stock entries** — One surface for receipts, issues, transfers, repack, manufacture pointers, and subcontract/customer flows, with additional inbound costs folded into valuation.
- **Opening stock** — Seed quantities and rates directly or via reconciliation.
- **Cancel/amend** — Submitted entries reverse through counter-entries; amendments create a linked new draft.

## Control

- **Reorder signals** — Nightly scan raises product replenishment triggers when projected stock breaches reorder levels.
- **Reservations** — Soft-hold order quantities against available stock, consume on delivery, release in bulk.
- **Putaway rules** — Priority- and capacity-driven bin assignment on inbound, with hard errors at full capacity.
- **Pick lists** — Location suggestions (oldest stock, nearest-expiry batches), stock refresh, submit-freeze, bulk reserve, and downstream gating.
- **Freeze window** — Backdated postings blocked except for the allowed role.

## Traceability

- **Serial numbers** — One row per unit from receipt to delivery, with warranty/AMC dates; never negative.
- **Batches** — Shelf-life masters with expiry blocking, splits, moves, and an expiring-batch feed for reminders.
- **Reconciliation** — Align book to physical counts (quantity, rate, or both), with serial/bundle awareness and CSV-ready bulk row import.
