---
title: Products
description: Centralized item master for goods and services.
icon: IconPackage
---

The Products module owns the single source of truth for every sellable, purchasable, stockable, or service thing the tenant offers — goods and services, raw materials, sub-assemblies, finished goods, subcontracted items, and fixed-asset items — with grouping, variants, barcodes, multi-UOM, and reorder defaults.
