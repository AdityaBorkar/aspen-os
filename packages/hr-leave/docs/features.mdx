Leave manages time off end to end — rules, balances, requests, holidays, and cash for unused leave.

## Leave setup

- **Leave types** — Define kinds such as sick or earned leave with their rules.
- **Leave years** — Create the periods leave is counted in.
- **Leave policies** — Bundle day limits per leave type and assign policies to employees.
- **Block busy dates** — Stop leave on blackout days.
- **Holiday calendars** — Maintain yearly holiday lists and the holidays in them.

## Balances

- **Grant leave** — Give leave days to an employee.
- **Check balance** — See remaining leave per type at a glance.
- **Leave history** — Browse every grant, deduction, and correction.
- **Manual correction** — Fix a balance with a recorded reason.

## Apply and approve

- **Apply for leave** — Request time off with dates and reason.
- **Approve or decline** — Managers approve (deducting balance) or decline with a reason.
- **Cancel leave** — Cancel approved leave and get the balance back.

## Extra earning

- **Time off for working a day off** — Claim compensatory leave for working on a holiday or weekly off.
- **Cash for unused leave** — Request encashment, get it approved, and track payout.
