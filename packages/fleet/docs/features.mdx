> The Fleet module is not built yet. The list below describes what a future Fleet module would cover — none of this is available today.

## Planned: vehicles

- **Vehicle register (planned)** — Keep one record per vehicle with registration and fitness details.
- **Documents and pollution checks (planned)** — Track insurance, fitness, and pollution certificates with renewals.

## Planned: people and trips

- **Driver assignment (planned)** — Assign drivers to vehicles.
- **Trip and usage log (planned)** — Record trips, mileage, and purpose.

## Planned: upkeep

- **Fuel log (planned)** — Track fuel fills and mileage.
- **Maintenance (planned)** — Schedule servicing and record repairs.
