> The CRM module is not built yet. The list below describes what a future CRM would cover — none of this is available today.

## Planned: customers

- **Customer records (planned)** — Keep one profile per customer with contact details and history.
- **Contact links (planned)** — Link the people you deal with at each customer.

## Planned: pipeline

- **Leads (planned)** — Capture and qualify new business enquiries.
- **Opportunity pipeline (planned)** — Track deals by stage through to won or lost.

## Planned: follow-ups

- **Activities and follow-ups (planned)** — Log calls, visits, and tasks against each customer.
- **Communication history (planned)** — See every message exchanged with a customer in one place.
