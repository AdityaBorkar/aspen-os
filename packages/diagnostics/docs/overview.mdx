Laboratory and radiology workflows, split out of `@aspen-os/healthcare`.

## Owns

- Lab test/panel masters, lab orders, barcode samples, versioned results,
  four-eyes authorization, critical acknowledgement, delivery, TAT and queue boards.
- Radiology bookings, check-in, reporting, authorization, and rescheduling.
- QC logging.

## Depends on

`healthcare` (`$dependencies = ["healthcare"]`) for the shared kernel:
branch/counter numbering (`healthcare_counter` via the local series step),
the open-encounter gate (`healthcare_encounter`), and canonical
`healthcare_observation` dual-writes. Storage tables stay defined in the
healthcare core; this module re-exports and pushes the same references.

## Events

Publishes `diagnostics.created`, `diagnostics.updated`, and
`diagnostics.authorized`.
