All workflows live under `src/workflows/diagnostics/` (one action per file)
and are composed as `diagnostics.*`:

- `addonTest`, `authorize`, `cancelOrder`, `collectSample`, `criticalAck`,
  `deliver`, `getOrder`, `orderLabs`, `panelCreate`, `processingStart`,
  `qcLog`, `queue`, `radioAuthorize`, `radioBook`, `radioCheckin`,
  `radioReportAttach`, `radioReschedule`, `receiveSample`, `resultEnter`,
  `sampleReject`, `tatReport`, `testMasterUpsert`.
