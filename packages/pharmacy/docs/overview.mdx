Pharmacy inventory and dispensing workflows, split out of `@aspen-os/healthcare`.

## Owns

- Items, batches (FEFO, expiry hard-block), prescription-linked sales with
  substitution guards, partial close, purchase orders, GRN verification,
  purchase invoices, credit/debit notes, stock transfers, reorder suggestions,
  and the stock ledger.

## Depends on

`healthcare` (`$dependencies = ["healthcare"]`) for gapless numbering
(`healthcare_counter` via the local series step). Storage tables stay defined
in the healthcare core; this module re-exports and pushes the same references.

## Events

Publishes `pharmacy.created`, `pharmacy.updated`, and `pharmacy.dispensed`.
