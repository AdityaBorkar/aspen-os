> The Reports module is not built yet. The list below describes what a future Reports module would cover — none of this is available today. (Reporting today lives inside each module, such as task summaries and leave histories.)

## Planned: see everything together

- **Cross-module dashboards (planned)** — Combine tasks, people, and files into one view.
- **Custom report builder (planned)** — Build your own reports with filters and groupings.

## Planned: share and repeat

- **Export (planned)** — Download reports as PDF, Excel, or CSV.
- **Scheduled reports (planned)** — Receive reports automatically on a timetable.
- **Shareable links (planned)** — Share a report through a link with access control.
