The Workspace module provides generic personal-workspace surfaces on `@aspen-os/platform`: **drafts** (saved, unpublished content with an optional approval lifecycle and threaded comments) and **dashboards** (named collections of metric/breakdown/list/embed widgets over a grid layout, with optional scheduled delivery) — plus the utility surfaces **pins, recent items, and quick search**. Saved filter views live in `@aspen-os/masters` (`p.masters.filterViews`); settings live in `@aspen-os/masters` (`p.masters.settings`).

Every data entity's **access is set by the user** at create/update time: `personal` (owner-only) or `global` (organization-wide within the tenant). Widgets and schedules **inherit** their parent dashboard's access.
