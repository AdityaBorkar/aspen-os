Workspace is your personal productivity layer — drafts, dashboards, saved views, and shortcuts that work across modules.

## Drafts

- **Start a draft** — Write content with a title, body, and notes before publishing.
- **Send for approval** — Submit a draft, approve it, send it back with a reason, or publish directly.
- **Reopen** — Bring a decided draft back for more work.
- **Copy a draft** — Duplicate a draft to reuse its content.
- **Discuss** — Comment on a draft and read the discussion.
- **Trash and restore** — Move a draft to trash, bring it back, or delete it permanently.

## Dashboards

- **Build a dashboard** — Collect charts and lists into one named view.
- **Rearrange** — Rename a dashboard and move its tiles around.
- **Copy, import, export** — Duplicate a dashboard, save it as a file, or recreate one from a file.

## Widgets

- **Add a tile** — Put a number, breakdown, list, or embedded note on a dashboard.
- **Refresh data** — Update a tile's numbers when the underlying data changes.
- **Edit or remove** — Change a tile's setup or take it off the dashboard.

## Scheduled delivery

- **Email a dashboard on a timetable** — Send a dashboard regularly to chosen recipients.
- **Pause or resume** — Temporarily stop scheduled delivery and restart it.
- **Track deliveries** — See each delivery timetable and record when one goes out.

## Saved filters

- **Save a view** — Store a filter, sort, and grouping for any area such as tasks, files, or notes.
- **Set a default** — Choose the view you see first for each area.
- **Copy a view** — Duplicate a saved view to adapt it.

## Shortcuts and search

- **Pin to your sidebar** — Keep drafts, views, and dashboards one click away.
- **Recently used** — Jump back to items you touched recently.
- **Quick search** — Search across drafts and dashboards as you type.

## Visibility

- **Private or shared** — Keep drafts, views, and dashboards to yourself or share them with the whole organisation.
