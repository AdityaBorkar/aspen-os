---
title: Masters
description: Polymorphic tenant master data module — contacts, addresses, bank accounts, integration connections, notes, entities, payment methods, and units of measure.
icon: IconDatabase
---

The Masters module owns eight tenant master-data entities: `contact`, `address`, `bankAccount`, `connection`, `note`, `entity`, and `paymentMethod` — polymorphic and scoped to an owner via `(entityType, entityId)` — plus `unitOfMeasure`, tenant-wide reference data. Owner types are `organization`, `branch`, `connection`, `contact`, or `entity`.
