Masters holds the shared reference data everything else uses — companies, branches, people, payments, units, and tags.

## Companies and branches

- **Register a company** — Add a customer, vendor, hospital, or other business party with its details.
- **Track status** — Mark companies active, inactive, or archived.
- **Add branches** — Record head offices, branches, warehouses, and stores in a tree.
- **View the tree** — See a branch with everything under it.

## People and addresses

- **Add a contact** — Save a business contact for a company, branch, or the whole organisation.
- **Update or park** — Edit details, park a contact with a leaving reason, or delete it.
- **Add an address** — Save postal addresses for any company, branch, or person.

## Payment options

- **Save a payment option** — Store bank, UPI, or card details for money in or out.
- **Pick a default** — Choose the preferred option for each use.
- **Enable or disable** — Turn a payment option on or off.

## Units of measure

- **Define a unit** — Add units such as kilogram or piece to a group.
- **Approve for use** — Publish a unit so everyone can use it, or retire ones you no longer want.
- **Pick a group default** — Choose the standard unit for each group.
- **Convert quantities** — Convert a quantity between units in the same group.

## Tags

- **Create tags** — Add shared labels with colours.
- **Tag any record** — Apply tags to companies and other master records.
- **Find by tag** — Pull up everything carrying a tag.

## External connections

- **Connect a service** — Save an outside service's address and credentials.
- **Test a connection** — Check that a connection still works.
- **Replace credentials safely** — Rotate the stored secret without downtime.

## Preferences

- **Company and personal settings** — Read and save company-wide and per-person preferences.
