Healthcare runs the day-to-day of an outpatient clinic — from registering a patient to billing them — plus clinical records. Specialty and ancillary care live in split-out modules: `@aspen-os/emr` (allopathy, dental, AYUSH, rehab, mental health), `@aspen-os/inpatient` (nursing, daycare stays), `@aspen-os/pharmacy`, and `@aspen-os/diagnostics` (lab and scans).

## Patients

- **Register a patient** — Create a patient file with a unique ID.
- **Avoid duplicates** — Check by phone or health ID whether the patient already exists.
- **Full visit history** — See every visit, prescription, and note in one timeline.
- **Family links** — Group patients from the same family.
- **Flags and allergies** — Mark VIP or high-risk patients and record allergies.
- **Follow-up recalls** — Put a patient on recall so they are reminded to return.

## Doctors and rooms

- **Manage doctors** — Add doctors with qualifications, fees, and weekly hours.
- **Find the next free slot** — See the earliest available appointment for a doctor.
- **Manage rooms and equipment** — Track rooms, chairs, and machines with live free or busy status.
- **Block time** — Mark doctor leave or room maintenance so nothing gets booked.

## Appointments and queue

- **Book a visit** — Book in-clinic or video appointments with doctor and room.
- **Reschedule or cancel** — Move a booking or cancel it.
- **Check in and queue** — Mark arrivals, hand out walk-in tokens, and call the next patient.
- **Follow-up slips and certificates** — Ask a patient to return, or issue a medical certificate.

## Visits and prescriptions

- **Consultation notes** — Record complaints, examination, and plan for each visit.
- **Diagnoses and vitals** — Add diagnoses and record blood pressure, pulse, temperature, and more.
- **Prescriptions** — Write and refill prescriptions, with interaction checks.
- **Lab and medicine orders** — Order tests, scans, or medicines straight from the visit.
- **Lock a visit** — Sign off a visit as final; later notes are added without editing the original.

## Speciality care

Speciality care lives in `@aspen-os/emr` (`p.emr`):

- **General medicine** — Track long-term conditions, vaccinations, and statutory registers.
- **Dental** — Chart teeth, plan staged treatments, give estimates, and track lab jobs such as crowns.
- **Ayurveda, homeopathy, and yoga** — Keep case sheets, schedule therapy sittings, sell packages, give diet charts, and run yoga batches.
- **Physio and rehab** — Start a rehab course, set goals, book sessions, share home exercises, and chart recovery.
- **Mental health** — Record assessments and screening scores, keep safety plans, book counselling, and track follow-ups.

## Daycare stays

Daycare stays live in `@aspen-os/inpatient` (`p.inpatient.residents`):

- **Admit for a day stay** — Admit with bed allocation and advance.
- **Daily care and rounds** — Record nursing notes, doctor rounds, and visitor logs.
- **Stay billing** — Add room and care charges and prepare the final bill.
- **Family updates** — Prepare and share progress summaries with the family.

## Pharmacy

Pharmacy lives in `@aspen-os/pharmacy` (`p.pharmacy`):

- **Stock medicines** — Add medicines and receive new batches.
- **Sell against prescription** — Bill medicines, handle part sales, and accept returns.
- **Order from suppliers** — Raise purchase orders, verify deliveries, and record invoices.
- **Move and correct stock** — Transfer stock between stores and fix counts.
- **Reorder and expiry alerts** — See what to reorder and what expires soon.

## Lab and scans

Lab and scans live in `@aspen-os/diagnostics` (`p.diagnostics`):

- **Order tests** — Order lab tests or book scans, including add-ons to an existing order.
- **Track samples** — Follow collection, receipt, processing, and reporting with barcodes.
- **Review and approve results** — Enter results, acknowledge critical values, and authorise reports.
- **Hand over reports** — Deliver reports to patients and monitor turnaround times.

## Billing

- **Raise a bill** — Build a bill from visits, services, medicines, and packages.
- **Collect payment** — Accept cash, UPI, or card, apply discounts, and settle fully.
- **Care packages** — Sell prepaid packages and use their credit on bills.
- **Dues and reports** — See who owes for how long, collections summaries, and GST exports.

## Nursing and records

- **Nursing tasks** — Turn doctor orders into nursing tasks with an urgency board (`p.inpatient.nursing` in `@aspen-os/inpatient`).
- **Charts** — Chart vitals, fluids, pain scores, and risk checks (`p.inpatient.nursing`).
- **Shift handover** — Prepare and sign off handovers between shifts (`p.inpatient.nursing`).
- **Patient records** — Search records, attach scanned files, share summaries, and issue discharge summaries (`p.healthcare.records`).

## Clinic setup and reports

- **Branches and templates** — Manage branches, note templates, and recall rules.
- **Standard lists** — Maintain shared lists such as complaints or diagnoses.
- **Custom reports** — Define, run, and download reports with controlled access.
