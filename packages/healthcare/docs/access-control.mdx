The Healthcare module declares its ACL via `defineAcl()`, which the platform merges from the module's `ModuleInfra.auth.acl` and applies to `AuthUnit` during `$prepareInfra()`.

## Resources & Operations

  allopathy: ["create", "delete", "read", "update"],

  ayush: ["create", "delete", "read", "update"],

## Elevated authority

The following semantics are elevated by design and must be enforced by the host app's roles:

- Billing discounts above 15% (`billing.discount-approve`) — maker-checker: the approver must differ from the discounter
- Lab and radio authorization (`diagnostics.authorize`) — four-eyes: the authorizer must differ from the result enterer
- Psych scale overrides and controlled-substance early refills (`psych.override`) — reason required, audited
- Clinical record access outside the care team (`records.breakglass`) — reason required, audited, publishes no event
