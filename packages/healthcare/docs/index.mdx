The Healthcare module is the OPD clinic backend built on `@aspen-os/platform`: patient registration, practitioner/facility/service masters, appointments and encounters, five EMR specialties, resident daycare stays, pharmacy, diagnostics, billing, nursing, clinical records, and operations. Staff, roster, attendance, leave, and payroll live in `@aspen-os/hr-core`, `@aspen-os/hr-attendance`, and `@aspen-os/hr-leave`.
