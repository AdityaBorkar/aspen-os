The Healthcare module is the OPD clinic backend built on `@aspen-os/platform`: patient registration, practitioner/facility/service masters, appointments and encounters, billing, clinical records, and operations. Specialty and ancillary care are split out: `@aspen-os/emr` (five EMR specialties), `@aspen-os/diagnostics` (lab and radiology), `@aspen-os/pharmacy`, and `@aspen-os/inpatient` (nursing and residents). Staff, roster, attendance, leave, and payroll live in `@aspen-os/hr-core`, `@aspen-os/hr-attendance`, and `@aspen-os/hr-leave`.
