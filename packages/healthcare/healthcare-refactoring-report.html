1. Executive summary

Recommendation (unchanged after re-analysis): shrink healthcare from 21 groups to 4 clinical modules, and dissolve ~2 shadow groups (operations, staff) by routing them through the platform modules that already own those concerns. Keep every clinical workflow; move only custody of generic capabilities (files, messages, tasks, schedules, masters, reports, compliance evidence, HR).
4 clinical modules
hc-foundation (patients, practitioners, facilities, services, pricelists, encounters, branches/counters) · hc-emr (allopathy, dental, ayush, rehab, psych) · hc-ancillary (pharmacy, diagnostics) · hc-care-ops (appointments, nursing, billing, residents, records-clinical)
8 integrations
comms (messaging), dms (files), calendar (scheduling/recall/reminders), tasks (nursing/order tasks), masters (value sets), compliance (evidence/retention/audit views), hr-* (staff), workspace + future reports (explorer/reports)

The two biggest wins are not clinical at all:

  1. Delete the parallel generic stack. Roughly one third of the module (the operations group at 19 actions, the staff group at 10 actions, plus messaging tables living in db-schemas/records.ts, the explorer-grant table living in db-schemas/staff.ts but owned by operations, explorer/report tables in db-schemas/operations.ts, and document handling in records/docs-attach.ts) re-implements what comms, dms, calendar, tasks, masters, compliance, hr-*, and workspace already own per AGENTS.md. That is where the module can get smaller without losing any clinic feature.
  2. Finish the internal unification that already started. Branch CRUD exists twice (admin/create-branch.ts vs operations/branches-create.ts, both publishing healthcare.branch_created per docs/events.mdx Branch table); pricelist writes exist twice (billing/pricelist-upsert.ts vs pricelists/*); “package” economics span services/define-package.ts, billing/package-sell.ts, ayush/sell-package.ts, dental/implant-milestone.ts, rehab/build-package.ts (but the shared math already lives in workflows/shared/package-lifecycle.ts — adopt it); boards/queues exist in five places but literals are already centralised in workflows/shared/board-query.ts (enforce it); consents exist in four stores but reads are already unified in workflows/shared/consent-lifecycle.ts (route all writers through it). Unifying each of these inside healthcare is a prerequisite to any extraction.

“Keep functionality similar” means: same workflow names available through a compatibility facade (p.healthcare.* keeps working as deprecated delegates), same healthcare.* topic names during transition (dual-publish where custody moves), same Valibot input shapes re-exported, same branch scoping, same gapless numbering, same audit-per-mutation and encounter-immutability guards — including the one read-that-publishes (records.encounterGet → healthcare.records_viewed) and the service_redeemed event that already works (do not regress either).

3. Why it hurts

3.1 One module, three different jobs

The same $name owns (a) clinic registration + EMR, (b) supply-chain and revenue execution (pharmacy PO/GRN, lab ops, invoicing), and (c) generic platform plumbing (messaging outbox, file store, explorer/reports, masters, compliance evidence, HR). The bounded-context map already flags the anomaly: every other domain module has 1–9 workflow groups; healthcare has 21 with 140 tables (.working-docs/BOUNDED_CONTEXTS.md context-map table).

4. Principles & non-negotiables

Do

  • Respect the AGENTS.md single surfaces: dms for files, comms for delivery/inbox, notes for generic notes, calendar for reminders (incl. task reminders via the existing task bridge — no second bridge).
  • One workflow action per file; compose public groups in the module router (existing convention, keep it). Note the three deliberate multi-export files (ayush followup-grid/yoga-attendance, dental implant-milestone, pricelists bulk/list/publish) — keep them as-is through the split.
  • Keep Valibot for domain input validation; do not introduce new Zod-based domain validation.
  • Keep one workflow group per bounded concept; move custody, keep clinical guards in healthcare.
  • Preserve branch scoping, gapless numbering, audit-per-mutation, the encounter-immutability gate, redeem → service_redeemed, and encounterGet → records_viewed verbatim.
  • Adopt the existing workflows/shared/* + workflow-steps/fetch-* helpers in every moved/duplicated flow instead of writing new ones.

Do not

  • Do not recreate drive / notifications / task_reminder parallels (explicitly banned in AGENTS.md).
  • Do not add an overloaded platform run(); tenancy stays class-time (Single/Shared/Isolated).
  • Do not replace Drizzle pushSchema() with migration files; do not add FKs where the design uses soft text references.
  • Do not commit .output/; do not hand-edit the better-auth schema; follow kebab-case filenames and the anti-slop lint rules.
  • Do not change clinical semantics to fit the split (return windows, refill caps, re-admin gates, four-eyes, maker-checker stay). Do not “fix” the non-broken service_redeemed path.
  • Do not “normalise” specialty clinic-capitalized text statuses into pgEnums, and do not silence the records_viewed publish to make “reads publish nothing” literally true.

Why four clinical modules and not 21 micro-packages? The five EMR specialties share one invariant (open-encounter gate via fetch-encounter.ts) and one payload shape; splitting them into five packages would quintuple the encounter-guard contract. The ancillary pair (pharmacy + diagnostics) shares barcode/batch/lot discipline and QC/authorization patterns. Foundation is the registration spine everything references by soft id. Care-ops is the visit-execution spine (schedule → nurse → bill → record). Four is the smallest split that removes the god-module without creating a distributed monolith.

9. Cross-cutting concerns you must not break

  • Branch scoping: every clinical table carries branch_id default "main"; workflows scope reads/writes to one branch. Any split must keep the branch filter in every query and keep the default. Do not reinterpret branchId as an org-tree id.
  • Gapless numbering: healthcareCounter + nextHealthcareSeries is transactional and shared (UHID, invoice/receipt/package, sale/return/PO/GRN/transfer, lab/radio, registers). If series leave the module, they must leave together with the same atomicity — never give each new module its own counter table with the same series names.
  • Audit + events per mutation (plus one read): writers call ctx.audit.write() then ctx.pubsub.publish(). The one exception is deliberate: records.encounterGet audits VIEWED and publishes healthcare.records_viewed (records/encounter-get.ts:131,137). Preserve the pairing, the topic names (dual-publish when custody moves), and this exception. Every produced topic still wants a subscriber (AGENTS.md pub/sub rule).
  • Encounter immutability: sign freezes the visit; post-sign changes go through addenda. All five specialty modules + nursing + diagnostics ordering depend on this. The shared fetch steps (workflow-steps/fetch-*.ts, 18 files; gate = fetch-encounter.ts) are the enforcement point — consolidate, don’t copy.
  • Elevated auth: billing:discount-approve, diagnostics:authorize, psych:override must survive with identical semantics (different-approver, reason-required, audited). ACL resource names should not be renamed until callers migrate.
  • Mixed status representation: shared lifecycles use pgEnums; specialty statuses stay clinic-capitalized text (docs/overview.mdx:76). A split that “normalises” these will silently change guard behaviour — don’t. Enforce imports from workflows/shared/board-query.ts.
  • Soft references: cross-entity links are text ids validated by guards, not FKs. New module boundaries must keep the same guard errors (patients/encounters/services/pricelists resolvers in workflow-steps/fetch-*.ts + workflows/pricelists/shared.ts), not replace them with joins.
  • Proven-correct event paths (new in v2): services.redeem → service_redeemed and records.encounterGet → records_viewed both work in code. Pin both with regression coverage before the split so the refactor can’t silently drop them.

12. Risks, trade-offs & open questions

  • Distributed guards: encounter-gating, four-eyes, and maker-checker currently run in-process via shared fetch steps. After the split they become cross-module contracts — slower to change, harder to keep atomic. Mitigation: keep the guard helpers in one shared clinical kernel (hc-foundation) that the other three depend on, not in each module.
  • Counter contention: one gapless series serving four modules + HR/masters needs the same transactional guarantees. Do not shard counters per module.
  • Event fan-out: 47 topics is already large; dual-publishing temporarily doubles it. Mitigation: dual-publish only moved topics, with a sunset note per topic in docs/events.mdx. records_viewed dual-publish is unnecessary (publisher doesn’t move) — just type + document it.
  • Practitioner vs employee: merging clinical identity into HR identity would leak employment lifecycle (onboarding/separation) into clinical authorship. Keep the soft link; do not merge the tables.
  • Resident scope creep: residents is daycare/long-stay, explicitly not IPD/ADT/OT/insurance (per docs/overview.mdx:7). A split is not permission to add ADT — keep the “no ADT/IPD” boundary in the new module’s docs.
  • Yoga/video scope: yoga batches and video sessions look separable but ride the same scheduling + encounter + billing rails as everything else. Keep them inside hc-emr/hc-care-ops with a calendar bridge; do not create wellness/telemedicine micro-packages.
  • Open questions for the team: (1) Should video/tele sessions converge on one session model across appointments + psych tele? (2) Should certificates, discharge summaries, and radio reports share one dms verification flow? (3) When the inventory/reports placeholders become real, does pharmacy stock-ledger and the explorer move again? (4) Who owns DEFAULT pricelist seeding — masters seed or billing seed? (5) Should the generic masking policy in records.notes-mask.ts become a notes primitive?
