HR Core keeps the company's people data — who works here, in which job, under whom — plus joining, moves, exits, and who can see what.

## Employee records

- **Add an employee** — Create a profile with joining date, department, designation, and manager.
- **Browse employees** — Search and filter everyone by department, status, and more.
- **Update a profile** — Change personal or job details.
- **Mark active, inactive, or left** — Track whether someone currently works here.
- **Groups** — Create employee groups and add or remove members.
- **Skills** — Record each person's skills and proficiency.

## Joining

- **Start onboarding** — Open a joining case for a new hire.
- **Joining checklist** — Add tasks such as document collection and complete them one by one.
- **Cancel onboarding** — Withdraw a joining case and its tasks.

## Promotions, transfers, and exits

- **Request a promotion** — Propose a new designation, approve it, and mark it complete.
- **Request a transfer** — Move someone between departments or branches with approval.
- **Start an exit** — Open an exit case with a checklist of handover tasks.
- **Exit interviews** — Schedule and record the exit conversation.
- **Final settlement** — Prepare, approve, and mark the final payout as paid.

## Jobs and org chart

- **Define a job post** — Create stable posts with department, branch, and headcount.
- **Place people in posts** — Assign employees, move them, or end an assignment while keeping history.
- **Org views** — See the company tree, a post's subtree, direct reports, teams, and peers.

## Departments and setup

- **Departments** — Create departments, nest them, assign heads, and view the full tree.
- **Job titles, grades, and employment types** — Maintain the standard lists used across HR.
- **Holidays** — Managed alongside leave (see HR Leave).
- **HR and payroll settings** — Adjust company-wide HR and payroll options.

## Access control

- **User accounts** — Link employees to logins.
- **Roles** — Create roles, decide what each role can do, and assign them to people.
- **Branch access** — Give or remove access to specific branches and check who can access what.
