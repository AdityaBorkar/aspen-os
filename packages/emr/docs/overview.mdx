Electronic medical records for five specialties, split out of
`@aspen-os/healthcare`: allopathy, dental, AYUSH, rehab, and psychiatry.

## Owns

- Allopathy: SOAP notes, exams, chronic logs, immunizations, triage, problem
  list, statutory registers.
- Dental: FDI charts, staged treatment plans, quotes, chair slots, lab jobs,
  implant milestones.
- AYUSH: case sheets, dual coding, repertorization, therapy packages and
  sittings, diet plans, yoga batches.
- Rehab: episodes, assessments, goals, packages, sittings, exercise sheets,
  discharge.
- Psychiatry: masked assessments, scales, risk/safety plans, counselling and
  tele sessions, withdrawal charts, relapse plans, controlled prescriptions.

Every specialty write gates on an open same-patient encounter owned by the
healthcare core.

## Depends on

`healthcare` (`$dependencies = ["healthcare"]`) for the open-encounter gate,
`healthcare_observation` / `healthcare_condition` canonical writes, and shared
patient/allergy reference tables. Storage tables stay defined in the
healthcare core; this module re-exports and pushes the same references.

## Events

Publishes `emr.allopathy_created/updated`, `emr.dental_created/updated`,
`emr.ayush_created/updated`, `emr.rehab_created/updated`, and
`emr.psych_created/updated`.
