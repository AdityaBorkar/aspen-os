Bedside and stay care, split out of `@aspen-os/healthcare`: nursing and
residents (daycare/long-stay).

## Owns

- Nursing: order-derived tasks, boards, vitals/EWS, intake-output, pain,
  risk screens, witnessed drug administration, handovers, triage tags,
  daycare sittings.
- Residents: admission, bed allocation, geriatric scores, polypharmacy
  reviews, daily logs, rounds, visit logs, stay charges and compiled bills,
  family summaries.

## Depends on

`healthcare` (`$dependencies = ["healthcare"]`) for the open-encounter gate,
`healthcare_observation` dual-writes, billing tables used by stay billing, and
gapless numbering. Storage tables stay defined in the healthcare core; this
module re-exports and pushes the same references.

## Events

Publishes `inpatient.nursing_created/escalated/updated` and
`inpatient.resident_created/updated`. The `tasks` bridge consumes
`inpatient.nursing_created`; `comms` consumes `inpatient.resident_updated`.
