Calendar keeps everyone's schedules, meetings, and nudges in one shared organization calendar.

## One calendar, three audiences

- **Organization** — visible to (and reminders delivered to) everyone.
- **Employee group** — scoped to an hr-core employee group and its members.
- **Person** — addressed to one user.

There is no per-user or per-team calendar to create, rename, or delete: the organization shares a single calendar, and scoping lives on each event and reminder.

## Events

- **Schedule an event** — Add a meeting or event with date, time, location, details, and an audience.
- **Repeat events** — Set daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly repeats.
- **Reschedule or edit** — Move an event or update its details.
- **Cancel an event** — Cancel while keeping a record of it.
- **Find events** — Browse by date range, audience, status, or keyword.

## Invitations

- **Invite people** — Add attendees to an event by email.
- **Track replies** — See who accepted, declined, or is still deciding.
- **Uninvite someone** — Remove an attendee from an event.

## Reminders

- **Set a reminder** — Get nudged about an event, task, file, or anything with a date.
- **Choose who is notified** — Organization-wide, one employee group, or one person.
- **Automatic task and document nudges** — Reminders for due tasks and expiring documents appear on their own.
- **See what's due** — Browse reminders by target, audience, or delivery state.
- **Change or cancel** — Update a reminder's time or turn it off.
