Calendar keeps all your schedules, meetings, and nudges in one place.

## Calendars

- **Create a calendar** — Set up a separate calendar for work, personal, or team use.
- **Browse your calendars** — See all calendars you have access to.
- **Choose your main calendar** — Pick which calendar new events go to by default.
- **Rename or update** — Change a calendar's name, colour, or timezone.
- **Delete a calendar** — Remove a calendar you no longer need.

## Events

- **Schedule an event** — Add a meeting or event with date, time, location, and details.
- **Repeat events** — Set daily, weekly, or custom repeats for recurring meetings.
- **See all repeats** — View every occurrence of a recurring event at a glance.
- **Reschedule or edit** — Move an event or update its details.
- **Cancel an event** — Cancel while keeping a record of it.
- **Find events** — Browse by calendar, date range, or keyword.

## Invitations

- **Invite people** — Add attendees to an event by email.
- **Track replies** — See who accepted, declined, or is still deciding.
- **Uninvite someone** — Remove an attendee from an event.

## Reminders

- **Set a reminder** — Get nudged about an event, task, file, or anything with a date.
- **Automatic task and document nudges** — Reminders for due tasks and expiring documents appear on their own.
- **See what's due** — Browse reminders by person or linked item, including ones now due.
- **Change or cancel** — Update a reminder's time or turn it off.
