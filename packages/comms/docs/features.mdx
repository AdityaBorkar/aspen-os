Comms handles everything you send or receive — inbox notes plus email, SMS, and WhatsApp delivery.

## Inbox notifications

- **Send a notification** — Notify someone in their inbox, with email or SMS attached if needed.
- **Check your inbox** — See your own notifications, unread first.
- **Mark read or unread** — Keep track of what you've seen.
- **Dismiss** — Clear notifications you have dealt with.
- **Unread count** — See at a glance how many need your attention.

## Sender accounts

- **Connect a sender** — Add your own email address, SMS number, or WhatsApp number to send from.
- **Test a sender** — Send a trial message to check it works.
- **Turn senders on or off** — Activate a sender for use or pause it.
- **Pick a default sender** — Choose the preferred sender for each message type.
- **Update credentials safely** — Replace a password or key without downtime.
- **Browse senders** — See all connected senders and their details.

## Reusable templates

- **Write a template** — Save a message with fill-in blanks for repeated use.
- **Browse templates** — Find and reuse saved messages.
- **Activate or retire** — Make a template available for sending or take it out of use.

## Your message preferences

- **Choose how to be reached** — Opt in or out of email, SMS, and other channels per message type.
- **Review your choices** — See all your messaging preferences in one place.

## Delivery tracking

- **Track sent messages** — See what was sent, delivered, or failed.
- **Retry a failed message** — Try sending again when delivery fails.
- **Workspace settings** — Adjust messaging defaults for the whole workspace.
