---
title: Comms
description: Channels, providers, notifications, and out-of-band message delivery.
icon: IconMessageSquareShare
---

The Comms module owns **channels** (sender endpoints), **host providers**, the **in-app notification inbox**, and the **out-of-band delivery outbox** for email, SMS, and WhatsApp. Tenant BYOC channels store credentials in the platform kvStore; host default channels materialize lazily from `comms_provider`.
