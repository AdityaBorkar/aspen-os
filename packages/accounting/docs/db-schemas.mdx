description: Tenant tables owned by the accounting module.

---

All tables are tenant-scoped with the `accounting_` prefix: accounts, fiscal years,
tax templates and rules, payment-term templates, quotations and items, sales orders and
items, delivery notes and items, sales invoices and items, material requests and items,
RFQs and items, supplier quotations and items, purchase orders and items, receipt notes
and items, purchase invoices and items, payment entries and references, journal entries
and lines, GL entries (append-only projection), assets, asset categories, asset
locations, depreciation schedules, and bank statement lines.
