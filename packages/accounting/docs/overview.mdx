## Goals

Single-tenant double-entry accounting with order-to-cash, procure-to-pay, asset register,
two reconciliation tools, and one-click financial statements.

## Non-goals

Cost centers, multi-company, multi-currency, VAT, pricing rules, blanket orders.

## Invariants

Drafts never post. Cancel reverses ledger per linked-document order. Submitted docs are
immutable (amend equals cancel plus new draft). One control account per reconciliation
scope. `update_stock` invoices are forbidden when a delivery or receipt already moved the
same quantity. Allocated amounts never exceed the smaller of outstanding and available.
Supplier invoice numbers are unique per supplier.
