Compliance tracks licences, certificates, and other papers through checking, expiry, and renewal — so nothing lapses unnoticed.

## Compliance papers

- **Track a paper** — Add a licence, certificate, permit, or policy document with its dates.
- **Send for checking** — Submit a paper for review.
- **Approve or send back** — Verify a paper or return it with a reason.
- **Mark as done** — Close out completed work on a paper.
- **Renew** — Archive the old paper and start its replacement in one step.
- **Assign work** — Hand a paper to someone to complete or to review.
- **Attach files** — Keep a scanned copy alongside the record.
- **Pause reminders** — Snooze nudges on a paper until a later date.
- **File away** — Archive papers that are no longer active.

## Repeating duties

- **Set up a repeating duty** — Define work that recurs, such as an annual tax filing.
- **Turn duties on or off** — Activate a duty so papers are generated, or pause it.
- **Preview upcoming due dates** — See future dates a duty will create.

## Review rules

- **Auto-assign reviewers** — Set rules so each kind of paper goes to the right checker.
- **Browse and edit rules** — View, change, or remove checking rules.

## Stay ahead of dates

- **Expiring and due lists** — Find papers expiring or due in the next few days.
- **Expired and overdue lists** — Find papers past their expiry or due date.
- **Escalation list** — Find papers that need a manager's attention.
- **Renewal history** — See the full chain of renewals for a paper.
- **Find by link** — Pull up all papers for one employee, vehicle, or branch.

## History and overview

- **Activity history** — See who did what on any paper or duty.
- **Download history** — Export the audit trail for reporting.
- **Health summary** — See overall compliance status at a glance.
