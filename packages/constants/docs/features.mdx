Constants holds the shared dropdown values every module uses — the same names and statuses everywhere, so records stay consistent.

## Organisation

- **Branch types** — Standard labels for sites, such as headquarters, office, warehouse, store, factory, or remote.

## People and companies

- **Contact types** — Standard labels for relationships, such as client, vendor, insurer, or partner.
- **Company types** — Standard labels for organisations, such as hospital, clinic, pharmacy, laboratory, or bank.
- **Record status** — Shared active, inactive, and archived states.
- **Payment methods** — Standard ways to pay (bank transfer, card, UPI, cheque) with active or archived states and inbound or outbound use.
- **Units of measure** — Standard groups such as weight, volume, length, time, and count.
- **External connections** — Standard connection kinds (API key, OAuth, webhook, database) with active, expired, or revoked states.

## Notes

- **Note types** — Standard labels such as call, email, meeting, issue, or general.

## Compliance

- **Document categories** — Standard labels such as tax, licence, safety, HR, legal, or vehicle.
- **Repeat frequencies** — Standard rhythms such as monthly, quarterly, yearly, or one-time.

## Messaging

- **Channel types** — Standard delivery routes such as email, SMS, WhatsApp, or push.
- **Notification states** — Shared unread, read, and dismissed states with normal, important, and urgent severity.
- **Delivery states** — Standard tracking from queued and sending through to delivered or failed.

## Countries

- **Country list** — Standard country codes used in addresses and forms everywhere.
