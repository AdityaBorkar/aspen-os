Notes are quick to capture and easy to find — private to you or shared with everyone, on their own or attached to your work.

## Capture

- **Jot down a note** — Save a quick thought with an optional title.
- **Note anything** — Attach a note to a contact, task, event, or keep it standalone.
- **Label as you go** — Mark notes by type such as call, meeting, email, or issue.

## Organise

- **Edit anytime** — Update the text, type, tags, or visibility of a note.
- **Tag notes** — Add tags so related notes stay together.
- **Private or shared** — Keep a note to yourself or make it visible to everyone.

## Find and clean up

- **Search and filter** — Find notes by tag, type, keyword, or the item they are attached to.
- **Delete** — Permanently remove notes you no longer need.
