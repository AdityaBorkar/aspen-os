Announcements let you share company news and updates with the right people, at the right time, and see who received them.

## Create announcements

- **Write an announcement** — Draft a company message with a title and details.
- **Edit a draft** — Change the text of a draft or scheduled announcement before it goes out.
- **Delete an announcement** — Remove a message you no longer need.

## Reach the right people

- **Choose your audience** — Send to everyone or narrow it to a branch, department, role, group, or specific people.
- **See recipients** — View exactly who an announcement was sent to.

## Schedule and publish

- **Publish now** — Send an announcement to everyone's inbox immediately.
- **Schedule for later** — Set a date and time for the announcement to go out automatically.
- **Cancel a scheduled send** — Stop a scheduled announcement before it goes out.
- **Pin to the top** — Keep an important announcement highlighted.
- **Archive and restore** — File away old announcements, and bring them back when needed.

## Track engagement

- **View read counts** — See how many people have read or acknowledged an announcement.
