Attendance tracks who worked, when, and for how long — including overtime and shift rosters.

## Daily attendance

- **Mark attendance** — Record whether an employee was present, absent, or on half day.
- **Browse records** — Look up attendance by person, date, or status.
- **Correct a record** — Fix a wrong entry.
- **Monthly summary** — See one employee's month at a glance, including late arrivals and early exits.

## Check-ins

- **Punch in and out** — Record check-in and check-out times, including location-tagged punches.
- **Browse punches** — Review raw check-in records per person or day.

## Correction requests

- **Request a correction** — Ask for a fix when attendance is missed or wrong.
- **Approve or decline** — Managers approve corrections or send them back with a reason.
- **Withdraw a request** — Cancel a correction you no longer need.

## Overtime

- **Define overtime rates** — Set rules for extra hours, weekends, and holidays.
- **Claim overtime** — Submit the extra hours you worked.
- **Approve and pay out** — Approve claims with automatic payout calculation, or decline with a reason.
- **Overtime totals** — See approved overtime for any period.

## Shifts

- **Define shift timings** — Set start and end times with grace periods.
- **Add work sites** — Save locations where shifts happen, with boundaries.
- **Assign shifts** — Place employees on shifts for a date range.
- **Request a shift change** — Ask to move to a different shift; managers approve or decline.
- **Weekly patterns** — Create repeating rosters such as Monday to Friday, and assign them to people.
