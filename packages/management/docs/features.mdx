Management looks after the platform itself — customer workspaces, the people in them, and the partners who support them.

## Customer workspaces

- **Set up a workspace** — Create a brand-new customer workspace.
- **Browse workspaces** — Find workspaces by plan or status.
- **Edit a workspace** — Update its name and profile.
- **Workspace logo** — Upload, display (signed URL), or remove the logo via Storage; the database keeps only the Storage key.
- **Activate or pause** — Approve a new workspace, temporarily freeze one, unfreeze it, or close one that left.
- **Login branding** — Look up a workspace by its address for the login page.

## Workspace members

- **Add members** — Invite someone into a workspace.
- **Change roles** — Update what a member can do.
- **Remove members** — Take someone out of a workspace.

## Service partners

- **Register a partner** — Add an implementation partner who looks after workspaces.
- **Partner logo** — Upload, display, or remove the partner logo via Storage (key stored on `service_provider.logo`).
- **Assign partners** — Attach a partner to a workspace or remove them.
- **Enable or disable** — Turn a partner on or off.
- **See assignments** — View which workspaces a partner supports and who works there.

## Organisations and users

- **Organisations** — Register and maintain platform-level organisations, including Storage-backed branding logos.
- **Users** — Create logins, browse by role or partner, change roles, move users between partners, and remove accounts.
