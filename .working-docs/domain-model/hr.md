# HR Domain Model

> Packages: `@aspen-os/hr-core` (`$name = "hrCore"`), `@aspen-os/hr-attendance` (`$name = "hrAttendance"`), `@aspen-os/hr-leave` (`$name = "hrLeave"`). Human resources — 8 sub-domains across 42 tables, all pushed as tenant schemas by their modules. Announcements live in `@aspen-os/announcement` (see `domain-model/announcement.md`), not here.

## Aggregates

### Employee (Aggregate Root, hr-core)

**Identity**: `id` (text, UUID, generated by `uuidv7` column type)

**Invariants**:

- `employeeId` must be unique
- `status` controls lifecycle (active → inactive → left)
- `reportsTo` forms organizational chart (hierarchical)

**Lifecycle commands** (via `p.hrCore.employee`): `create(input)`, `update(id, patch)`, `getById(id)`, `getByEmployeeId(id)`, `list(filters?)`, `deactivate(id)`, `activate(id)`, `markAsLeft(id)`, `getOrganizationalChart()`.

**Supporting surfaces**: employee group management (create/update/delete groups, add/remove/list members), skill map management (create/update/delete, list by employee).

### Attendance (Aggregate Root, hr-attendance)

**Identity**: `id` (text, UUID, generated by `uuidv7` column type)

**Invariants**: One record per `(employeeId, date)`; `workingHours` derived from check-in/out; `lateEntry`/`earlyExit` minutes tracked; `isHalfDay` flag; shift association. Attendance requests follow correction workflow (pending → approved/rejected).

**Lifecycle commands** (via `p.hrAttendance.attendance`): `create(input)`, `update(id, patch)`, `deleteRecord(id)`, `getById(id)`, `list(filters?)`, `getSummary(filters?)`, `createCheckin(input)`, `deleteCheckin(id)`, `getCheckinById(id)`, `listCheckins(filters?)`, `createAttendanceRequest(input)`, `updateAttendanceRequest(id, patch)`, `approveAttendanceRequest(id)`, `rejectAttendanceRequest(id)`, `deleteAttendanceRequest(id)`, `listAttendanceRequests(filters?)`, `getAttendanceRequestById(id)`.

### Leave (Aggregate Root, hr-leave)

**Identity**: `id` (text, UUID, generated by `uuidv7` column type)

**Invariants**: Leave applications follow approval workflow (pending → approved/rejected → cancelled). Ledger entries track allocations/consumption; encashment and compensatory leave are separate workflows. `getLeaveBalance(employeeId, leaveTypeId?)` computes current balance.

**Lifecycle commands** (via `p.hrLeave.leave`): leave types/periods/policies/policy assignments/policy details (create/update/delete/get/list), leave allocations, applications (create/update/approve/reject/cancel/get/list), block lists, adjustments, encashments (request/approve/reject/mark paid), compensatory leave (create/approve/reject/update/delete), ledger entries (list).

### Lifecycle (hr-core)

**Identity**: `id` (text, UUID, generated by `uuidv7` column type)

**Invariants**: Onboarding (completion tracking), promotions (with salary revision), transfers (between departments/branches/companies), separation (exit date + resignation date). Promotions and transfers follow an approval workflow (pending → approved → completed, or rejected); onboarding and separation progress directly to completed and can be cancelled.

**Lifecycle commands** (via `p.hrCore.transition`): onboardings (create/update/complete/get/list/remove), promotions (create/approve/complete/reject/get/list/remove/update), transfers (create/approve/complete/reject/get/list/remove/update), separations (create/update/get/list/remove). Completion side effects write back onto `employee` (department/branch/company) in the same transaction.

### Overtime (Aggregate Root, hr-attendance)

**Identity**: `id` (text, UUID, generated by `uuidv7` column type)

**Invariants**: Configurable overtime types (rates, multipliers for holidays/weekends); overtime slips follow approval workflow (pending → approved/rejected).

**Lifecycle commands** (via `p.hrAttendance.overtime`): overtime types (create/update/delete/get/list), overtime slips (create/update/approve/reject/delete/get/list), `getOvertimeSummary(filters?)`.

### Shift (Aggregate Root, hr-attendance)

**Identity**: `id` (text, UUID, generated by `uuidv7` column type)

**Invariants**: Shift types (start/end times, grace periods, auto-attendance), shift locations (geofencing), shift assignments, shift requests (approval workflow), and shift schedules (weekly day-of-week assignments).

**Lifecycle commands** (via `p.hrAttendance.shift`): shift types/locations/assignments/requests/schedules/schedule assignments (create/update/delete/get/list + approve/reject requests + deactivate assignments).

### Access (HR RBAC sub-domain, hr-core)

**Invariants**: Role-based access control within HR module — permissions, roles, and branch-wise access controls for HR users. user's effective permissions are union of their roles' permissions, scoped by `hr_user_branch_access`.

**Lifecycle commands** (via `p.hrCore.access`): users (create/update/delete/get/list), roles (create/update/delete/assign-to-user/remove-from-user/list), permissions (create/delete/assign-to-role/remove-from-role/list), branch access (grant/revoke/update/check/list), permission checks (`hasPermission`, `getUserPermissions`, `getAccessibleBranches`, `getUserRolesForBranch`).

### Setup (org structure sub-domain, hr-core + hr-leave)

**Invariants**: Department (hierarchical via `parentDepartment`, has `manager`/head), HrSettings, PayrollSettings. Holiday + HolidayList live in hr-leave schemas (see `p.hrLeave.config`).

**Lifecycle commands** (via `p.hrCore.config`): `departments` (create/update/delete/get/list + `move`, `setHead`, `tree`, `subtree`); `hr` and `payroll` singletons (get/update). Holidays via `p.hrLeave.config`: `holidays`, `holidayLists` (create/update/delete/get/list, list-by-list).

### Payroll (reporting surface, hr-core)

**Invariants**: No payroll tables — `export` aggregates `employee` rows with soft reads of hr-attendance (`attendance`, `overtime_slip`) and hr-leave (`leave_application`, `leave_encashment`) tables. Missing attendance/leave tables degrade to empty rows with a note, never a failure. `payableDays = present + work_from_home + half_day * 0.5 + on_leave + approvedLeaveDays`; `lopDays = absent + half_day * 0.5`; cross-month overtime prorated by overlapping days; rounding follows `payroll_settings.rounding`.

**Lifecycle commands** (via `p.hrCore.payroll`): `export({ month, company?, branch?, department?, employeeId?, leavePeriod? })` — read-only, publishes no events.

### Announcement — extracted

Broadcasts moved to `@aspen-os/announcement` (`$name = "announcement"`, `$dependencies = ["hrCore"]`): 1 workflow group (14 actions), 2 tenant tables (`announcement`, `announcement_recipient`), 6 events, 1 ACL resource, no cron. See `domain-model/announcement.md`.

## Invariants & Business Rules

1. **Scheduled cron jobs** — `hr.daily-attendance-sync` (`0 1 * * *`, hr-attendance) and `hr.daily-leave-accrual` (`0 0 * * *`, hr-leave) are registered in `$prepareRuntime()` and unregistered in `$cleanup()`. Announcement scheduling is workflow-only (no cron). hr-core registers no runtime resources.
2. **Schema placement** — all module tables are pushed as tenant schemas (`control_plane_schemas` is empty in every HR module). Announcement's 2 tables are tenant-scoped (see `domain-model/announcement.md`).
3. **Approval workflows** — leave applications, attendance requests, overtime slips, shift requests, promotions, and transfers follow pending → approved/rejected transitions enforced in workflow. Onboarding and separation complete directly (no approval step).
4. **Org structure** — reporting and grouping derive from `employee.department` + `employee.reports_to` only; designations and positions/assignments were removed. The org chart is the `reportsTo` tree; department deletion is blocked while child departments or active employees exist.
5. **Transition side effects** — promotion approval/completion patches `employee.department`; transfer completion patches `employee.branch`/`department`/`company`; separation records the exit date and employee status change happens via employee workflows.
